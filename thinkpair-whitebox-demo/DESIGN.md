# ThinkPair Whitebox Noise Room

## Style Prompt

A restrained vertical short-video prototype for ThinkPair. The scene is a white-gray psychological lab for information overload: ordinary faceless person, floating screens, tool buttons, course labels, transparent maze, blank action paper, book, review notebook, and a small fluorescent-green judgment core. The mood is clean, tense, quiet, and reflective rather than motivational. The video should feel like a repeatable account workflow, not a one-off poster.

## Colors

- Warm white background: `#f4f1ea`
- Lab gray: `#d8d5ce`
- Ink: `#1d211f`
- Muted text: `#656b67`
- Glass line: `#aeb5af`
- Signal green: `#b7ff3c`
- Soft shadow: `rgba(29, 33, 31, 0.14)`

## Typography

- Primary Chinese display and subtitles: `Microsoft YaHei`, `PingFang SC`, sans-serif.
- Small system labels: `Cascadia Mono`, `Consolas`, monospace.
- Display text is heavy and large; captions are readable and calm.

## Motion Rules

- The room breathes slowly; information noise moves inward before being cleared.
- Transitions use a white-gray scan wipe, like a lab shutter passing over the frame.
- Green appears only when judgment is being recovered.
- No jump cuts. Each scene enters with staggered motion and resolves into the next.

## What NOT To Do

- No PPT slides, cards, information graphics, or diagram-heavy layouts.
- No red-black "狠人" pressure style, black-gold success style, or cyberpunk UI.
- No dense text walls. Text must be sparse enough for mobile viewing.
- No realistic celebrity/person footage or stock-photo atmosphere.
- No selling tools; AI tools are treated as one form of noise.
